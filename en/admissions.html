Costs and scholarships

Real cost per student

USD 5,040

This is what it really costs to train one student. Our volunteer network covers USD 3,040 of that cost.

Direct enrollment

USD 2,000

For those who enroll directly, without applying for a scholarship. Covers the full program and the final certificate.

With a 92.5% scholarship

USD 150

If you apply for the 92.5% scholarship and are admitted, you pay a single contribution of USD 150 (S/ 530 in Peru).

Payment methods

  • From any country, including Peru: PayPal or Stripe, payable to AYP Business School.
  • From Peru only: bank transfer to Aprende y Prospera LatAm (RUC 20609198720). Bank details are provided once your admission is confirmed.

Project Lab short courses have a contribution of USD 20 with scholarship (S/ 30 in Peru).

Student Wellbeing Unit

If you cannot cover the USD 150 contribution, the Student Wellbeing Unit will review your case confidentially. It considers your financial situation, your academic record, your commitment to completing the program and your commitment to volunteer in return.

What we ask for

  • Socioeconomic form
  • Letter of intent
  • Three references
  • One-page CV
  • Commitment to volunteer 2 to 6 hours per week
  • Good performance and commitment to complete the program

Conditions

  • Additional scholarships are limited and awarded based on need, merit and commitment.
  • Volunteering is required to receive the certificate; if it is not completed, the waived amount must be paid.
  • Anyone who does not complete the program must pay the waived amount.
  • Incomplete or late applications are not considered.

Volunteer roles during training: teaching assistant, classroom assistant, academic tutor, learning resources, virtual forum support, academic management support, virtual events, databases and forms, administrative support, digital management, program ambassador, community moderation, content creation and social media. Some roles require a related university degree.

Refund and credit policy

Effective June 26, 2025. Applies to all courses, specialized programs and workshops offered by AYP Business School and Aprende y Prospera LatAm, online or in person.

Enrollment payments are non-refundable

Your payment covers only a fraction of the program's real cost, which is heavily subsidized thanks to our volunteers. For this reason, enrollment is not refunded, whatever the reason for not continuing: a schedule change, or personal, work or health reasons, among others.

Instead, you can request an academic credit

  • Email info@aprendeyprospera.org no later than two weeks after the official start of classes.
  • The credit equals the amount you paid.
  • You can use it for another Aprende y Prospera program within 24 months of its issue date.
  • It is personal: it cannot be transferred to another person or combined with other promotions or scholarships.
  • Only one credit is granted per student per enrolled program.

If we cancel a program

If we cancel a course or program for operational reasons or force majeure, you can transfer your enrollment to another program of equal value or receive a full refund of what you paid, with no penalty.

Exceptional cases

The Academic Office reviews force majeure situations individually, such as a serious illness or the death of an immediate family member. Supporting documentation is required.

Frequently asked questions

Do the programs award academic credit or degrees?

No. They are non-credit continuing education programs. When you complete one, you receive a digital certificate issued by AYP Business School.

Are classes live?

Yes. Classes are synchronous on Zoom, in the evening and one Saturday a month. Activities and assessments take place in the Campus Virtual (Moodle).

How much does a specialization cost?

The real cost is USD 5,040. Direct enrollment is USD 2,000, and if you are admitted to the 92.5% scholarship, you pay a single contribution of USD 150 (S/ 530 in Peru).

What if I can't pay the USD 150?

You can ask the Student Wellbeing Unit to review your case. Depending on your situation, your contribution can drop to USD 100, 50 or 0 in exchange for 2, 4 or 6 hours of volunteering per week.

Who do I pay?

From any country, AYP Business School via PayPal or Stripe. From Peru, you can also pay by bank transfer to Aprende y Prospera LatAm.

Can I apply from the United States?

Yes. Our programs are open to professionals in Latin America and to Hispanic and Latino immigrants in the United States.

What do I need to study?

A device with an internet connection, a camera for live classes and availability during the program schedule.
