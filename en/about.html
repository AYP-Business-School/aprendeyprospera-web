One global vision, two organizations working together

Aprende y Prospera is a global nonprofit educational organization. Since 2020 it has expanded access to quality education and economic opportunity for professionals in Latin America and Hispanic and Latino immigrants in the United States.

Mission and vision

Mission

To develop comprehensive, accessible, high-quality experiential training and empowerment programs for Latin Americans and Hispanic immigrants around the world that help them manage and grow their entrepreneurial initiatives, and to offer professional certifications that help them find employment and secure their economic and personal wellbeing.

Vision

A world where Latino people, wherever they live, have equal access to quality education and to opportunities to grow economically, reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to stronger communities.

From a hackathon to a community in 20 countries

  1. 2020MIT HackathonAprende y Prospera began informally in June 2020, during the MIT Latin America vs. COVID hackathon, as a response to unemployment and the lack of access to quality education in Latin America.
  2. 2021Aprende y Prospera LatAmIt is formally incorporated in Peru as a nonprofit civil association, offering online courses, coaching, workshops and virtual forums.
  3. 2022First specializationWe launched Project Management, with experiential learning within a continuing-education framework.
  4. 2023AYP Business SchoolIt is founded in Massachusetts, U.S., as a 501(c)(3) organization so that programs also reach Hispanic and Latino immigrants. Second specialization: E-commerce and Digital Marketing with AI.
  5. 2024New programsThird specialization: Instructional Design and Digital Didactics. Project Lab (Circular Entrepreneurship and Innovation) is launched, along with the first edition of the Business Plan for Entrepreneurs course.
  6. 2025Sustainability modelShared-responsibility model: scholarship recipients make a modest contribution, and those who cannot pay can contribute volunteer hours.
  7. 2026Applied Agentic AINew specialization in Applied Agentic AI for Automation and Business Leadership, and the second issue of AYP Impact Review.

Today we are a community of 130 volunteers in 20 countries.

One vision, two organizations

Massachusetts, U.S.

AYP Business School

501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in Massachusetts, U.S. (EIN 88-3474800). It is responsible for academic programs, enrollment, certification and all financial management.

Lima, Peru

Aprende y Prospera LatAm

Nonprofit civil association in Peru (RUC 20609198720) and where the initiative began. It runs projects in Latin America and handles registration and payments for students who enroll from Peru.

Both organizations are independent, each with its own board of directors, and they work in coordination, each with defined responsibilities.
