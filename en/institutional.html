Academic quality

We have a quality assurance team that runs satisfaction surveys, monitors participation and prepares reports for each program. We are working to meet NECHE standards for non-credit education providers (Recognition of Non-Credit Providers).

Curriculum design

An academic innovation and instructional design team reviews every course.

Continuous evaluation

End-of-course surveys and tracking of attendance and participation.

Qualified faculty

Professionals with industry experience in Latin America and the United States.

Participation rules

When you enroll, you accept the Terms and rules for participating in Aprende y Prospera courses. In summary:

  • Attend live classes with your camera on
  • Submit assignments in the Campus Virtual and complete the satisfaction survey
  • Work as a team and with respect
  • Meet your payment or volunteering commitments
  • Be 18 or older: this is the minimum age for all our programs

Non-discrimination policy

AYP Business School and Aprende y Prospera LatAm do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national or ethnic origin, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability, immigration status, marital status or any other status protected by law, in admission, access to their programs, scholarship awards, volunteering or employment.

Privacy

We use the data you share with us only to manage your application, enrollment, certification and our communications. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to update or delete your information by writing to us.

We want everyone to be able to use our website and our programs. If you find a barrier or need a reasonable accommodation to study, write to us and we will help.
