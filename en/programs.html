Experiential learning

Small businesses, nonprofits and civil society organizations bring real projects or challenges, and our student teams solve them with the support of mentors.

For students

  • Individual reflection on your own project: strengths, areas for improvement and growth strategies
  • Teamwork on a partner organization's business or real challenge
  • Delivery of actionable strategies, prototypes or recommendations

For companies and organizations

  1. Contact the program
  2. Define a project or challenge
  3. Agree on goals and expectations
  4. Work with mentors and students
  5. Receive strategies, recommendations or prototypes
